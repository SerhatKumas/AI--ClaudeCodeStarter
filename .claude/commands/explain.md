Explain: **$ARGUMENTS**

Help me understand this code without changing it.

1. Locate the relevant code and read it along with its key collaborators.
2. Explain, from high level to detail:
   - **Purpose** — what it does and why it exists.
   - **Flow** — how data/control moves through it, step by step.
   - **Key pieces** — the important functions/types and their roles (cite `file:line`).
   - **Dependencies** — what it relies on and what relies on it.
   - **Gotchas** — edge cases, non-obvious behavior, or sharp edges.
3. Use a small diagram or numbered walkthrough if it aids clarity.
4. Note anything that looks buggy, risky, or confusing — but don't fix it unless I ask.

Keep it concrete and tied to the actual code, not generic theory.
